# Market scan for Ivy

This is a practical summary of the platforms that mid-sized companies most commonly use today, plus a few newer or fast-rising options. The goal is to help you decide where to host Ivy, which ITSM tools to support, which knowledge sources to connect, and which intranet platforms are worth evaluating.

## Recommended console coverage

For a product console, I would keep the initial list to about 6–8 primary options and 2–3 emerging ones:

- **Collaboration**: Teams, Slack, Google Chat, Zoom
- ITSM: ServiceNow, Jira Service Management, Freshservice, Zendesk
- Knowledge: SharePoint, Confluence, Google Drive/Docs, Notion/Box, ServiceNow Knowledge
- Intranet: SharePoint + Viva, Staffbase, Simpplr, Unily/LumApps

## Practical recommendation for Ivy

If your goal is a bot that can answer HR and employee questions across tickets, knowledge, and internal portals, the most common and practical stack for a mid-market company is:

1. Host Ivy in Teams or Slack if the company already uses one of those tools.
2. Connect it to ServiceNow or Jira Service Management if the company already has an ITSM system.
3. Pull knowledge from SharePoint, Confluence, Google Drive, or ServiceNow Knowledge.
4. Use SharePoint + Viva, Staffbase, or Simpplr as the intranet layer if the company wants a more polished employee experience.

## Bottom line

- Most-used today: Teams, Slack, ServiceNow, Jira Service Management, SharePoint, Confluence.
- Best mid-market fit for a bot like Ivy: Teams or Slack + ServiceNow or Jira + SharePoint/Confluence + a modern intranet layer.
- Fastest-growing newer options: Microsoft 365 Copilot/Teams agents, Atlassian Rovo, ServiceNow AI Agents, Slack AI, and knowledge platforms such as Glean and Guru.
