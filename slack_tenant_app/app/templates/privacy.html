Privacy Policy

What we collect

  • Slack workspace details — workspace (team) ID and name, captured when an admin installs IvvY.
  • Installing admin's email address — used to identify the account owner and for support correspondence.
  • OAuth access tokens — for Slack and for any knowledge source you choose to connect (Jira, Confluence, SharePoint, G Suite).
  • Content from connected sources — issue summaries, page titles and body text, used to build the answers IvvY gives in Slack.
  • Questions and answers — the questions users ask IvvY and the answers it returns.

Why we collect it

Solely to operate the service: to identify your workspace, to read the knowledge sources you explicitly connect, and to answer your team's questions in Slack. We do not sell your data, and we do not use your content to train or fine-tune machine-learning models.

How it is stored and isolated

Every record is scoped to your workspace's tenant ID, and each tenant's content is stored under a separate storage bucket. Data is transmitted over TLS 1.2 or higher.

Retention and deletion

Your data is retained while IvvY is installed. Uninstalling IvvY from your Slack workspace revokes its access; to have stored data and tokens deleted, contact us using the address below and we will action the request within 30 days. You may also request a copy of the data we hold for your workspace.

Your rights

You may request access to, export of, or deletion of your workspace's data at any time by emailing the contact below. We respond to data requests within 30 days.

Use of AI

IvvY uses a large language model to generate answers from the content you connect. Model output can be inaccurate or incomplete and should not be relied on as authoritative. Your content is not used to train models.
