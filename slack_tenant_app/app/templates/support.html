Installing IvvY

  1. You need Slack admin rights — Slack only shows the install option to admins.
  2. Click Add to Slack and approve the requested permissions.
  3. IvvY installs into the workspace you're currently signed in to.
  4. Optionally connect Jira, Confluence, SharePoint, or G Suite. You can skip this and IvvY will answer using its built-in knowledge.

Removing IvvY

Uninstall IvvY from your Slack workspace's app management settings. That immediately revokes its access to your workspace. To also have stored data and tokens erased, email us and we'll action it within 30 days.

Common issues

  • "You don't have permission to install apps" — your Slack workspace restricts installs to admins. Ask an admin to install IvvY, or to approve your request.
  • A connected source shows "Failed" — the OAuth token may have expired or been revoked at the source. Use Try again on that tile to reconnect.
  • Answers look out of date — use Read again on a connected source to re-read its current content.

A note on AI answers

IvvY generates answers with a large language model. Output can be inaccurate or incomplete — verify anything consequential against the source material.
